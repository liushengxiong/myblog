Short Bio

I'm Leo Liu (刘盛雄), a business practitioner with over 10 years of experience in marketing and operations. I hold dual master's degrees—an MBA and an MEM—from Shanghai Jiao Tong University.

My career spans multiple industries including fast-moving consumer goods, internet technology, and B2B e-commerce. I've spent years studying consumer behavior, brand growth strategies, and business model innovation.

Currently, I'm fully immersed in exploring personal growth pathways in the AI era, content entrepreneurship opportunities, and the viability of the "company of one" model. I believe AI technology is reshaping how individuals create value—and that's the most exciting opportunity of our time.

Why AI

In 2023, the emergence of ChatGPT made me deeply realize: AI is no longer a distant future technology, but a present force that's transforming how everyone works and lives.

As a marketing and operations professional, I see tremendous potential for AI in content creation, data analysis, and user insights. More importantly, I realize AI provides individuals with unprecedented leverage—one person can now accomplish what previously required an entire team.

This sparked my passion for deeply studying AI, and led me to ask: In this new era, how can ordinary people maximize their personal value with AI?

Why This Website

I built this website for three core reasons:

1. Document and Reflect In the rapidly evolving AI era, continuous learning and reflection are essential. This site is where I record my learning insights, practical experiences, and deep thinking. Writing helps me better understand and internalize knowledge.

2. Build a Personal Brand In an age of information overload, personal brand is one of the most valuable assets. Through consistent, high-quality content output, I aim to establish my expertise in AI applications and personal growth.

3. Connect with Like-minded People I believe the future belongs to those who embrace change and never stop learning. Through this website, I hope to connect with more kindred spirits to explore the opportunities of the AI era together.

As Derek Sivers says, "Writing is thinking refined." This website is where I refine my thinking and share value.

Future Plans

My future plans revolve around three directions:

Content Creation Continuously produce high-quality content about AI tool applications, personal growth methodologies, and business model analysis. I plan to publish at least one in-depth article per week.

Project Practice Transform theory into practice by building AI-powered personal workflows and automation systems. Explore the practical feasibility of the "company of one" model.

Community Building Gradually build a reader community, organize online sharing sessions and discussions, and create an ecosystem for mutual learning and growth.

I look forward to meeting you on this journey.
